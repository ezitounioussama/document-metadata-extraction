AI Trends 2025
A Technical Report

Prepared by Dr. Sarah Lee
Published 2025-05-01
Institute for Applied Machine Learning

This report surveys the state of artificial intelligence as of early 2025. It covers the
continued scaling of large language models, the shift toward smaller specialised models that
run on local hardware, and the growing regulatory attention on transparency.

Three themes recur throughout. First, deep learning architectures have stabilised while the
competitive frontier has moved to data quality and evaluation. Second, retrieval-augmented
generation has become the default pattern for grounding model output in verifiable sources.
Third, ethics in AI has moved from an academic concern to a compliance requirement, driven by
the EU AI Act and comparable legislation elsewhere.

The report concludes that organisations adopting AI in 2025 should invest less in model
selection and more in evaluation infrastructure.
