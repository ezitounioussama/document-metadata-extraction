Northwind Logistics Opens Automated Sorting Facility

Northwind Logistics today announced the opening of its automated parcel sorting facility on
the outskirts of Lyon. The site processes up to 40,000 parcels an hour and is the company's
first fully automated hub in France.

The facility uses computer vision for label reading and a fleet of autonomous floor robots for
routing. Northwind says the site will cut average delivery times in the region by a day.
