Climate Change and Agriculture

By John Smith · 15 September 2024

Shifting rainfall patterns are already changing which crops are viable in southern Europe. This article reviews the evidence and looks at what sustainable farming practices can realistically absorb.

Farming practices under pressure

Drought-resistant cultivars and no-till methods reduce exposure, but both require up-front investment that smallholders rarely have. Environmental policy has so far focused on emissions rather than adaptation.

Policy gaps

Subsidy structures still reward volume over resilience. Until that changes, sustainability remains a cost rather than an incentive.
